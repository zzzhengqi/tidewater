Tip

Press H for the settings: the time of day, the sun's path, the wind and the waves are all yours to change.

Press F to fly the free camera over the island and the sea; F again to walk.

Walk into the sea to swim; hold C or look down to dive over the reef.

Press E at the boat to board, at the wheel to take the helm, and again to step away and walk the deck.

Press P for photo mode: the interface goes, the view stays.

Press R for the equipment wheel. Pick the rod to fish: hold the left mouse button to wind up, release to cast.

Watch the bobber. A few nibbles, then it's pulled under: click the left mouse button that moment to set the hook.

Fishing, the minimap and the tips can each be switched off under H → Gameplay.
